# Construction Plan

## Origin and coordinate conventions
- Build origin is the world coordinate `(0, 0, 0)`.
- Positive X is east; negative X is west; positive Z is south; negative Z is north.
- The campus center is aligned to the origin.
- Ground level is documented as the build surface at Y=0 and construction begins at Y=2 for most above-ground features.
- The underground archive begins at Y=-12 and extends down to Y=-24.

## Campus footprint
- Total horizontal footprint: approximately 200 x 200 blocks
- Plaza and center library footprint: approximately 60 x 60 blocks
- Department ring: distributed around the library on a radius between 44 and 80 blocks
- Research wings: east, west, north, and south wings around the main axis
- Observatory: positioned east-north-east of the library
- Gardens: beyond the outer department ring and around the plaza perimeter

## Main structures

### Central library
- Center: (0, 0)
- Shell: X from -25 to 25, Z from -25 to 25
- Main height: Y from 2 to 24
- Dome ring: Y from 18 to 24
- Glass dome center and rib structure around the upper crown

### Plaza
- Center: (0, 0)
- Footprint: X from -18 to 18, Z from -18 to 18
- Fountain basin: center at (0, 3, 0), water basin 8x8
- Path connections extend to all major wings and entrances

### Knowledge department ring
- Department block footprints are 8x8 to 12x12 depending on cluster
- West cluster: X 48 to 60; Z -36 to 36
- East cluster: X -48 to -60; Z -36 to 36
- North cluster: Z -48 to -60; X -36 to 36
- South cluster: Z 48 to 60; X -36 to 36
- Diagonal outer zones: X/Z ±60 to ±72

### Research wings
- East research wing: X 40 to 62, Z -18 to 18
- West research wing: X -40 to -62, Z -18 to 18
- North research wing: Z -40 to -62, X -18 to 18
- South research wing: Z 40 to 62, X -18 to 18

### Observatory
- Origin: X 72, Z -56
- Tower footprint: 16x16 around the central support and observation room
- Height: Y 2 to 32
- Observation deck at top around Y 26-32

### Underground archive
- Entry stairs: from (0, 2, 28) to (-12, -12, 28)
- Archive volume: X from -66 to -38, Z from 10 to 42, Y from -12 to -24
- Historical vault rooms and shelving along the inner corridors

### Gardens
- North lawn: Z -70 to -90
- South lawn: Z 70 to 90
- East lawn: X 70 to 90
- West lawn: X -70 to -90
- Tree rows on the outer ring perimeter

## Staged build approach
- Build foundation
- Build plaza and central library
- Build department ring and research wings
- Build observatory
- Build underground archive
- Build gardens and finishing details

## Clear area recommendation
- Recommended clear area: at least 200 x 200 blocks centered on the player spawn or chosen origin
- Avoid building directly inside another structure if the world already contains a large village or base

## Safe build rules
- The campus is generated by function calls and does not autodetect terrain height
- The project expects a flat or cleared area and documents the build origin explicitly
- The build is centered on the current world coordinates and should not be used in a world with dense nearby structures without review
