## Department placement summary
The departments are distributed across the campus in logically grouped clusters:
- west science and humanities ring
- north life and earth sciences
- east engineering and applied research
- south social and computational fields
- diagonal wings for future-facing and interdisciplinary projects

## Identification method
Each department area is marked with a visible armor stand label and a dedicated room volume with matching materials and study furniture.
